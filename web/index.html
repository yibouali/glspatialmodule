The learning module

Read the landscape with data.

Historical redlining offers one lens for examining how policy shaped cities. In the notebook, you will work with the archived neighborhood boundaries and explore a workflow for comparing them with vegetation information from satellite imagery.

The goal is to practice spatial analysis and interpret evidence in context—not to treat a map or a single metric as a complete explanation of present-day environmental conditions.
